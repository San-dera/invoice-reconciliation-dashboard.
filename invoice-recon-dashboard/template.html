Checks before you rely on this

  • Statement credits are positive numbers; the system stores them as negatives. They must be subtracted.
  • One payment key (24673_1) appears twice. Match on payment number plus PO number.
  • Late charge rules are an assumption: use the controls above to test them.
  • Report dates are in 2010 but the title says April 2020.
